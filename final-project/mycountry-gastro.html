Gastronomy of the Dominican Republic

Dominican cuisine is a historically developed culinary fusion linked to the evolution of the Dominican Republic, with European, African, and indigenous influences. The main ingredients used are rice, meats, legumes, tubers, greens, vegetables, seasonings such as chicken broth, tomato paste, liquid seasonings, and, to a lesser extent, fish and seafood.
